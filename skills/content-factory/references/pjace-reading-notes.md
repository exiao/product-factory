## Application decisions

- Eric's comedy/education preference is explicit guidance for his AI-led social production, not a research finding. The archive contains additional genres; it does not compare their effectiveness experimentally.
- Apply authored premise, recognizable tension, earned payoff, product connection, references, editorial selection, and audio/visual cohesion. Keep these conditional on the actual brief.
- Preserve real testimony and process evidence. Acknowledge fiction without requiring documentary truth from fictional stories. Do not manufacture backstage reality as evidence of a human shoot.
- Do not import creator hype, exact throughput, tool rankings, specific providers, retry quotas, image-grid dimensions, or upscaling advice as fixed skill requirements. Reverify technical details when executing.
- These notes support instruction changes, not a claim that the changes improve watch time, comedy, trust, or sales. That requires finished work and audience evidence.
