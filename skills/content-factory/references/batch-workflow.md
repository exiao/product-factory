# Batch workflow

Use for delegated content or paid-creative production. Sequence by dependencies; reuse settled decisions and skip irrelevant steps. A request for copy alone ends with copy, while a request for finished video ends with an inspected playable export.

## Establish the batch

Reuse or briefly state the product, audience situation, objective, destination, available sources, deliverables, constraints, and finish line. Choose a small feasible batch when quantity is unspecified and state that assumption. Do not impose a universal weekly quota or silently combine unrelated products and audiences.

Separate the content objective from the business outcome: attention, education, conversation, activation, lead, or purchase. Identify what can actually be measured. Check existing assets and performance before asking the user to collect more. If account access or product facts are missing, proceed on independent production work with explicit limits.

## Select material and concepts

Find the useful tension, insight, demonstration, or story in the source. Retain source paths and timecodes for selected excerpts. Keep quotations faithful and distinguish customer language from suggested copy. Verify current factual claims when necessary. Bound research to an unresolved production decision; enough evidence to choose a concept is a reason to start making it.

For each shortlisted concept, keep a compact brief: audience/situation, hook, payoff, proof, format, intended response, and source. Paid briefs also need product connection and destination/offer alignment. Compare materially different reasons to care, not merely synonyms. Avoid fake precision in predicted winning probabilities.

Develop a representative hook-through-payoff execution for an uncertain direction before expanding it. If the core result is unconvincing, change the premise or evidence before adding effects or more versions. The agent can select and continue when delegated; preserve explicit user selection checkpoints.

## Produce through the requested finish line

Route writing, images, footage editing, narration, and sound to the relevant available specialists. Read their operational instructions before using tools. Use actual product captures for demonstrations and inspect what they show. Keep source files and editable project material when useful for requested iteration; avoid unnecessary documentation.

A rough voice memo can become an authored script, but do not invent experiences in the speaker's voice. Reference material can inform pacing or composition without copying expression or implying reuse rights. Use media the user owns, has permission to use, or that is appropriately licensed for the intended use; mark unresolved rights before publication.

When recording is necessary, give a precise shot or line request with intended duration and purpose. Do not substitute synthetic spokesperson footage, a storyboard, or a static asset for requested filmed media without the user's agreement. Complete unaffected deliverables while the dependency is pending.

Adapt each derivative to its audience and placement: opening, context, pacing, crop, captions, and next action. Verify current destination specifications before final export. Use available tools; building a generator, dashboard, or integration is a separate scope decision, not the default solution to producing a batch.

## Inspect and deliver

Review the final rendered artifact at its intended viewing size. For video, inspect playback, timing, cuts, captions, and audible mix when audio access is available. For static media, inspect legibility, crop, layout, product accuracy, and unwanted generated details. Check factual wording, destination consistency, and applicable platform requirements using specialist guidance. State any sensory or platform check that could not be performed.

Fix material defects within scope and inspect the affected final export again. Successful rendering proves file creation, not persuasive quality. Qualitative review establishes a judgment with limits, not audience validation or ad-platform acceptance.

Deliver the files/previews, matching copy and destinations where relevant, the recommendation, and any remaining dependency. Use concise filenames to distinguish variants. A self-contained review page is useful for multiple visual options but is optional. Do not deliver a calendar in place of requested media.

If publication or campaign activation is authorized, use the available destination tooling and verify the final post, schedule, or campaign state. On an ambiguous write result, inspect destination state before retrying to avoid duplicates. Otherwise finish the reviewable package and present only the specific remaining decision. Asset creation and this skill's invocation do not authorize additional spending.

## Learn from results

Read performance when requested or already part of the delegated outcome. Recurring monitoring requires an explicit recurring request. Keep asset identity, observation window, audience/placement, spend, sample size, and downstream outcome together using the existing records.

Separate reach/attention from clicks, destination conversion, and customer value. Missing conversion data is unknown. Investigate tracking, delivery, offer, or destination issues before treating weak conversion as a creative failure. Unequal spend and small samples limit comparisons; do not manufacture a winner or causal claim from descriptive results.

Recommend which idea to retain, which variable to change, and which new angle to test. Preserve successful evidence and revise one meaningful variable for controlled comparisons when practical; exploratory batches can compare distinct concepts with their confounds stated. Organic response is a source of paid-test hypotheses, not a guarantee. No fixed view threshold, expiry date, or arbitrary volume establishes readiness to scale.

If iteration is authorized, produce the revised assets through inspection rather than stopping at recommendations. Stop at the agreed batch or review point; do not automatically launch, increase budgets, or build a perpetual system.
