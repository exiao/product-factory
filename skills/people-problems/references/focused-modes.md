# Focused people-problem modes

Read this reference only for `people-problems question`, `people-problems check`, or `people-problems reframe`.

## Question

Name the assumption, the statement or decision it supports, its evidence label, and the observation that would support or challenge it. Add an uncertainty or confidence label only when the supplied evidence supports one; do not manufacture precision. Keep self-report, observed behavior, quantitative evidence, analyst inference, and synthetic reaction distinct. Do not turn a workaround into proof of dissatisfaction.

## Check

Trace each claim to the supplied source or stable source ID. Record what the source establishes, what it does not establish, and the next check when the gap matters. Mark absent, inaccessible, or unobserved evidence as unknown or untested. Do not add research or rewrite the problem unless requested or already authorized within a broader task.

## Reframe

Show the smallest local annotation and before/after statement needed to improve human language, solution agnosticism, why, or certainty. Preserve audience, situation, priority, source attribution, and any stable IDs. Label composed first-person language as synthesis or hypothesis. A local request does not require a full problem dossier.
