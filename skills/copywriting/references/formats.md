# Format patterns

## Short blurb

A useful starting structure is promise, mechanism, takeaway: name an existing difficulty; say who does what; name the decision, tool, or artifact the audience gains. Use only supported details. A calendar invite can instead be the action, preparation, and link. Do not pad short surfaces into a landing page.

## Direct-response video

Map spoken copy and visible proof together. A possible sequence is curiosity, problem, discovery/demo, validation, and next action. Fit beats to the actual duration through a read-through rather than assuming fixed seconds per sentence. A shorter ad may omit a problem beat; a demo may need more screen time than speech.

Useful variants: a real pain-point discovery, a sourced skeptical customer story, a product unboxing, a demonstrated lifestyle use, or a tip list leading to a product demonstration. Claimed expertise and first-person results must belong to a real supported speaker; an AI presenter does not turn invented testimony into evidence. Label fictional dramatizations appropriately. Preserve useful pauses and breath rather than removing all silence.

Reference ads can reveal a structure worth exploring. How long an ad has run does not establish profitability, and popularity does not validate its claims. Adapt the structure to the actual product, audience, and evidence.

## Quiz or personalized funnel

Ask questions that affect a real result. Keep answer options understandable, offer uncertainty where appropriate, and avoid unnecessary personal questions. Explain how answers influence recommendations. Loading messages should describe actual processing; do not invent waiting periods or pretend generic results are individualized. Use verified proof and honest next-step copy.

## Compare variants

Hold the offer and surface constant when comparing wording. Label each variant by angle, count characters when a real limit matters, and state assumptions that might change the recommendation. For an experiment, define the audience, outcome metric, assignment, and observation period from the actual traffic and decision. Do not import universal conversion benchmarks or declare a winner from tiny samples.
