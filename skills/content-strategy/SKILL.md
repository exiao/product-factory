# Content strategy

Start with the business or audience outcome, product truth, reader/viewer, channel, resources, and decision horizon. Reuse supplied positioning and research. Match strategy to awareness and intent rather than assuming every consumer is cold or every B2B audience is ready to buy.

For decisions about what is trending now, use [$last30days](../last30days/SKILL.md) or [$trend-research](../trend-research/SKILL.md) with actual dated sources. Stable editorial planning can start from existing customer evidence without an obligatory trend hunt. Distinguish audience language and observed behavior from creator anecdotes, engagement counts, and vendor claims.

For Eric's AI-led content, start with comedy or education as the preferred test lanes; preserve a requested alternative genre. Plan around a specific laugh or learning payoff rather than generation novelty. Distinguish AI-assisted authentic material, clearly fictional storytelling, and synthetic depictions that would be mistaken for lived experience. Use [AI creative judgment](../content-factory/references/ai-creative-judgment.md) when selecting these directions. Creator reach anecdotes do not establish that a genre converts or that other genres fail.

Sources: [Veo 3 Gold Rush (2025-05-23)](https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/don-t-miss-the-veo-3-gold-rush) describes expectation and comic reversal; [Fantasy Sports Scroll (2025-09-11)](https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/call-me-crazy-but-this-is-the-future-of-espn-and-will-be-a-1m-ai-brand-next-year) combines useful commentary with recurring fictional characters. These are creator accounts, not controlled performance evidence. The comedy/education default and authenticity safeguards are Eric’s preference and our editorial synthesis, not findings proved by these posts.

Use three diagnostic lenses, not mandatory gates:

- **Outcome and mechanism:** connect the feeling, identity, or practical benefit to what the product really does. High-intent readers may need capability, compatibility, and pricing first.
- **Product connection:** for conversion-oriented creative, show how the product enables the demonstrated result. Broader educational or community content can have value without making the product indispensable.
- **Substance without adjectives:** name a concrete difference, useful insight, or shift. If differentiation is weak, report it; do not silently expand a content task into redesigning the product.

Choose a small set of pillars and repeatable formats tied to distinct audience needs. For each proposed item or series record audience/intent, thesis, format, proof needed, product connection, channel, next action, production effort, and success measure. Provide actual prioritized briefs rather than a catalogue of frameworks. Read [references/angles-formats-and-series.md](references/angles-formats-and-series.md) when choosing angles or planning a series. When channel choice or a repeatable acquisition unit is part of the strategy, read [references/channel-fit-and-acquisition-unit.md](references/channel-fit-and-acquisition-unit.md).

For B2B, education, and events, consider decision support: comparisons, migration details, objections, demonstrations, and concrete takeaways people can use or forward. For an event, plan pre-event promise, useful live moments, and post-event recordings/recaps when in scope. Do not fabricate credentials, customer stories, statistics, or artificial urgency.

Hand off or continue requested production with [$writer](../writer/SKILL.md) for Eric-authored voice, [$hooks](../hooks/SKILL.md) for openings, [$copywriting](../copywriting/SKILL.md) for conversion structure, [$video-script](../video-script/SKILL.md)/[$video-direction](../video-direction/SKILL.md) for video, and [$character-creation](../character-creation/SKILL.md) for recurring characters. Use only capabilities actually available. Strategy-only work does not require producing every asset or creating new user-owned tasks.

Plan cadence from available capacity, quality, channel behavior, and evidence. Do not mandate daily volume, account warming, a fixed creative-expiry period, or universal view thresholds. Read [references/distribution-and-learning.md](references/distribution-and-learning.md) for measurement and the organic/paid feedback loop. For a launch plan, read [references/launch-phases-and-checklist.md](references/launch-phases-and-checklist.md) and adapt its phases and checklist to the audience, resources, and authorized channels. Scheduling, posting, hiring creators, launching ads, or recurring monitoring require that scope in the user's request; preserve existing authorization without asking again.

Deliver the prioritized strategy and concrete next batch, with assumptions, evidence gaps, metrics, and a review point. Use a durable calendar or brief file when the task needs one; avoid compulsory documents for a narrow strategy question.
