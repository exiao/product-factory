# Content critic role brief

Use this as an optional independent review assignment. Read the brief before the artifact. Judge the exact supplied version and return findings rather than editing the asset under review.

For a film, assess story clarity, motivation, performance, pacing, continuity, and the intended listening/viewing experience. For an ad, assess whether its audience can recognize the promise, whether it delivers that promise, and whether the creative choices suit the actual brand. Do not force every piece to be broadly viral, emotional, or stylistically identical.

Tie each material finding to a line or timestamp and shot ID where available, its consequence, and a concrete correction. Distinguish brief violations from optional taste judgments. Name a rubric gap rather than disguising it as an existing rule.

Report ready, needs revision, or insufficient evidence for the requested review. Frame samples support frame-level findings only; missing full audiovisual inspection prevents a complete-film verdict. Identify the reviewed version and what was actually inspected. Use [$evaluate-content](../../../evaluate-content/SKILL.md) for prose review and `video-direction` for treatment context.
