# Story editor role brief

Use this as an optional bounded assignment. Read the treatment, draft, selected direction, and affected shot rows. Diagnose character wants, obstacles, choices, scene causality, audience knowledge, emotional turns, dialogue, and continuity. Separate story defects from camera, performance, and sound choices.

Return scene-by-scene notes or a revised draft as requested. Each significant change names the affected scene/shot and the problem it solves. Preserve the chosen film, voice, and unrelated scenes. A text read-through is not evidence of spoken performance.

Use scene sections for authoritative script revisions and identify affected scene/line IDs and shot references. Propose changes unless editing the shared production document is assigned; do not maintain a competing dialogue copy in shot records. Deliver the notes/draft, source version, affected shot IDs, and any factual or media-inspection limits. The director resolves overall creative direction.
