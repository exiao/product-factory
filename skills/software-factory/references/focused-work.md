# Focused work

Read only for recommend, compare, trace or critique. Use the existing artifact and sufficient surrounding context. Preserve the person, task, source material, agreed direction and evidence labels. A target may be a passage, panel, flow, criterion or project decision. If missing context materially prevents the operation, identify the gap and complete what is possible; do not start a full discovery exercise.

## Recommend

Identify the uncertainty most likely to change the next decision. Recommend the smallest useful operation on the current artifact and state its expected result. Offer alternatives only when they lead to meaningfully different learning. This is advice; do not execute a proposed experiment merely because it was recommended. Resume an already-authorized larger task when applicable.

## Compare

Hold actor, starting material and desired outcome constant across the supplied approaches. Show what each consumes, does and changes; the burden it removes and adds; the hardest assumption; and a reason to reject it. Use agreed outcome criteria when available. Do not manufacture new audiences or alternatives to a settled choice.

When usefulness depends on output quality, compare actual worked samples if available within scope. Clearly label manual, curated or simulated results and unavailable execution. A scenario explaining potential value is not evidence of reliable automation or demand. Recommend the smallest discriminating test and stop after the requested comparison unless further work is authorized.

## Trace

Follow the selected promise or artifact through: problem → supporting source → intervention → outcome criterion → observed result. This is a mapping of evidence, not a requirement to create missing artifacts. Mark absent links, conflicting versions and unsupported jumps at the affected claim. Preserve stable IDs and attribution. A synthetic reaction cannot substantiate demand; a screenshot cannot prove backend execution; a successful component check cannot alone prove the whole outcome.

Return a compact mapping with supported, inferred or missing links and the next observation needed. Do not silently rewrite criteria, fill gaps with invented sources, or declare completion from artifact presence alone.

## Critique

Review how the idea is supposed to help the person, rather than its visual polish. Mark the exact sentence, storyboard panel or transition where the promised benefit lacks an explanation. Show what the person supplies, what the intervention contributes, what the person must still decide or do, and what changes in the resulting state. Identify hidden setup, interpretation or correction work. Distinguish a reproduced defect from a reasoning hypothesis.

Return an annotation, the smallest clarification or local revision proposed, and a plausible test of the causal claim. Inspection does not authorize applying the change. If revision is requested, show before/after and preserve the surrounding scenario; do not turn the review into screen design.

## Finish

Keep findings adjacent to their targets and show only the relevant changes. Include evidence limits and one useful next move where needed. No numeric score, permanent report or additional skill call is required merely to finish an operation.
