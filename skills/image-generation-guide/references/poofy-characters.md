# Poofy characters

Use this treatment for fluffy 3D characters or plush avatar families. Adapt [Agentara's reference](https://github.com/agentara/skills/blob/main/skills/aigc/fluffy-poofy-3d-characters/SKILL.md), inspected on 2026-09-29. Inspect its [example avatar](https://github.com/agentara/skills/blob/main/skills/aigc/fluffy-poofy-3d-characters/assets/reference-avatar.png) before using it as a style input. Treat the image as design-language guidance, not an identity to duplicate.

Brief a dominant soft body shape, small limbs, tactile fibers, and a quiet smooth face recessed into the fluff. Keep facial features simple and expressions subtle. Light the material so its texture and contact shadow read clearly. Respect the requested palette, setting, pose, and crop.

For sets, plan different body proportions, face openings, fiber treatments, and appendages. Color swaps and accessories alone do not establish variety. Preserve common facial construction and lighting. For a sheet, make count and placement explicit and keep each full silhouette readable.

Inspect count, structural diversity, fuzzy-body/smooth-face contrast, limbs, and unwanted text. Correct the observed defect. Use the guide's built-in generation and delivery workflow. The source's background, grid size, and example count are optional choices.
