# Edit anatomy

Create a beat sheet with one row per cut or distinct event:

```text
beat | time | shot/framing | spoken/on-screen text | caption style |
motion/transition | overlay | audio
```

Extract patterns rather than copying every timestamp: hard cut plus punch-in per sentence, a word-pop caption treatment, a whoosh on transitions, or a silence before the close. Apply those rules to the user's footage and message. Location variety and cut-on-the-line usually affect energy more than decorative camera moves. Full-bleed framing, readable captions, and deliberate black cards should be choices supported by the measured reference.

The reference supplies editing grammar, not the original script, footage, music, brand marks, or distinctive creative expression. Keep the new piece original and use licensed or user-supplied media.
