# Hook patterns

These are writing mechanisms, not measured effectiveness rankings. Bracketed fields need evidence before publication.

| Mechanism | Shape | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research synthesis | After comparing [items], [finding] | Actual comparison and finding |
| Experience with constraint | How [person] achieved [result] despite [constraint] | Attributed outcome and constraint |
| Cautionary tale | [Choice] caused [consequence]. Here is what changed. | Real event, no invented autobiography |
| Practical resource | [Resource] for [specific job] | Resource actually delivered |
| Counterintuitive observation | [Expected approach] missed [observed factor] | Support for the contrast |
| Small intervention | [Change] improved [outcome] | Evidence of the improvement, bounded claim |
| Behind the scenes | What happened when we tested [thing] | A real test and result |
| Recognized tension | [Reader situation] makes [task] difficult | Credible audience context |
| Bounded series | Episode [N]: [this installment's payoff] | Actual recurring format and numbering |

Choose specificity that the material supports. Do not borrow famous names to imply endorsements, invent quantified losses, or call ordinary information a secret. Use episode numbers rather than day streaks when publishing cadence is uncertain. A series number complements a recognizable format; it cannot substitute for a useful installment.
