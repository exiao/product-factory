# Written hooks

Start from the actual content, audience, surface, and payoff. Read supplied evidence before making claims. The opening should make relevance clear and give a credible reason to continue. Tension, a specific result, a useful artifact, or a surprising observation can do this; drama is not compulsory.

Use hooks for written entry points. Use [$video-script](../video-script/SKILL.md) or [$video-direction](../video-direction/SKILL.md) for audiovisual cold opens and retention decisions. For Eric-authored text, apply [$writer](../writer/SKILL.md)'s voice guidance; the user's requested tone and format take precedence.

Never invent a personal experiment, achievement, number, date, role, testimonial, or finding to strengthen an opener. An example pattern is not evidence. Use sourced specifics, write around missing facts, or visibly mark a draft placeholder. Do not turn financial product capabilities into promises of returns or privileged knowledge.

Choose a few distinct mechanisms appropriate to the material. See [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md) for a compact pattern bank. For B2B or educational work, a recognizable consequence or concrete takeaway often works better than accusation or shock. For high-intent/search surfaces, directly naming the capability can be the strongest opening.

Coordinate headline, subtitle, and opening sentence: each should add information rather than repeat or indefinitely delay the payoff. Name a tangible takeaway when one exists, especially for events and lead magnets; do not force every story into an artifact promise. Deliver the promised value in the body.

Respect the requested number of options. For open exploration, give a small distinct set and recommend one with a brief reason. For a single rewrite, give the line. Avoid formulaic rationale on every variant unless helpful. Do not claim a hook is proven or assign win probabilities without real testing.

Check length and truncation in the actual surface when available. LinkedIn line breaks and preview cutoffs vary with device and layout; a two-line, 55-character heuristic is not a platform guarantee. Preserve essential meaning even if the preview truncates. No automatic publishing or obligatory follow-up question.
